How to use this in a lesson

Intention to outcome. Set the character's intention first, then change one performance variable at a time. The studio shows when your delivery serves the intention and when it pulls against it, so pupils can argue why a choice works rather than guessing.

Design without a rig. The lighting stage models lantern type (soft fresnel, hard-edged profile, wide flood, punchy PAR), angle (front, side, back, top, footlight), position, colour and intensity. Back light silhouettes; side light sculpts; cold colour and low intensity isolate. Build a state, read the mood line, then justify it in Eduqas design vocabulary.
